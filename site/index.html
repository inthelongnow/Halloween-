Bio

Queens kid.
Genre icon.

30+Years on screen
60+Film & TV credits
4Saw films

Born in Queens, New York, Dina Meyer broke out with a recurring role on Beverly Hills, 90210 before landing the female lead opposite Keanu Reeves in Johnny Mnemonic.

What followed was a run through some of the most-loved genre films of the era: Kara in Dragonheart, the fearless Dizzy Flores in Paul Verhoeven's Starship Troopers, Commander Donatra in Star Trek: Nemesis, and Detective Allison Kerry across the first four Saw films.

On television she became the first live-action Barbara Gordon as Oracle in Birds of Prey, and has appeared in Friends, NCIS, Point Pleasant, Castle, and Scorpion. She is a regular on the convention circuit, where she meets the fans who have kept these characters alive for decades.

Off set she cooks, she writes, and she answers your questions. That's what this site is for.

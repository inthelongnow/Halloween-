# Jteve Sobs style guide

Jteve Sobs (@jtevesobs) is a short-form AI music-video maker. The look is luminous techno-mysticism: sacred geometry, microscopy, and glass architecture run through heavy glitch, beat-synced into optimistic "slop accelerationism." Not grim cyberpunk. More machine-elf wonder.

He has said the stills are mostly his own Midjourney library, the tracks are Suno, and the cut, motion, and beat sync are an LLM edit pass he calls the slopcannon.

## What the frames actually do

### Subjects

Radial sacred forms: mandalas, rose windows, nautilus sections, exploding crystal eyes, trees of life. Those get fused with circuitry, DAW timelines, MRI grids, and retro software chrome.

Recurring figures:

- Pale, freckled faces with circuit patterns projected on the skin and glowing eyes.
- Silhouettes (a rider, a classical bust) reaching into a data-storm.

Organic and machine stay mixed: metal flowers on boards, a glass city inside a shell, a cosmic library under a black-hole ring.

### Palette and material

- Black void plus acid neon: cyan, magenta, electric blue, hot pink, acid green.
- Surfaces read as liquid glass, chrome, and iridescent crystal.
- Often a false-color science look: fluorescence microscopy, thermal, scan.

### Treatment

- Chromatic aberration, RGB split, pixel-sort, and datamosh are structural, not a filter on top.
- Compositions snap between a single exploding radial and a grid of mutating variants of the same form (spirals, cells, brains, hexagons).
- Hard cuts land on the beat.
- The mood stays upward: "never doom," eschaton as a party.

## Prompt skeleton

Keep this scaffold and swap the subject:

> [your subject], sacred geometry fused with circuitry, liquid glass and chrome, radial explosion on pure black, fluorescence-microscopy palette (cyan, magenta, acid green, hot pink), heavy chromatic aberration and pixel-sort, crystalline eye or mandala as the focal point, scientific imaging mixed with psychedelic render, luminous not horror

Variants that sit in his lane:

- Close-up pale freckled face, RGB circuit projection on skin, glowing blue eyes, glitch shards at the frame edge.
- Nautilus cross-section containing a glass city, technical illustration mixed with psychedelic render.
- Silhouette riding toward a pixel-sorted rainbow form made of data, blue sky, dust, hard color split.

## Where the Halloween twist fits

His system is a motif language (radial glass, grids, false-color, beat cuts), not a fixed character. Halloween and creature work drops in cleanly if you keep the materials and color and change the organism:

- Crystalline goblin eye as the mandala center.
- Zombie skin as iridescent circuit-flesh.
- Sea creature as a nautilus of liquid chrome.

Or keep your own character pipeline and only borrow the grade, the grid montages, and the cut rhythm.

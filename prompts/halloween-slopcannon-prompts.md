# Halloween prompts in the Jteve Sobs lane

Every prompt below keeps the shared base and swaps only the organism. Paste a subject line, then append the base.

## Shared base

> sacred geometry fused with circuitry, liquid glass and chrome, radial explosion on pure black, fluorescence-microscopy palette (cyan, magenta, acid green, hot pink), heavy chromatic aberration and pixel-sort, scientific imaging mixed with psychedelic render, luminous not horror

Rules that keep it in his lane:

- Black void background. No fog, no graveyards, no moonlight.
- The creature is a specimen or a relic, never a threat. Wonder over dread.
- One radial focal point per frame: an eye, a mandala, a cross-section.
- Glitch is part of the object, not a post layer. Say "pixel-sorted" about the flesh, not about the image.

**Pumpkin rose window**

**Spider web hex-grid**
> orb-weaver spider at the center of a web that is also a printed circuit board, silk traces glowing magenta, eight crystalline eyes each reflecting a different mandala

## Grid frames (mutating variants of one form)

Use these as the "montage" beat. Same form repeated in a 3x3 or 4x4 grid, each cell a mutation.

- Nine goblin eyes, each iris a different sacred-geometry pattern, same slit pupil, same black void.
- Sixteen pumpkin rose windows, carving pattern evolving cell by cell from simple triangle to full mandala.
- Grid of zombie skin samples under false-color microscopy, decay progressing left to right as circuit density increases.
- Grid of bat skeletons as retro software chrome icons, wings as spiral variants.
- Grid of candy corn as iridescent crystal cells, hex-packed, color-shifting cyan to hot pink.

## Figure frames (silhouette into the data-storm)

- Headless horseman silhouette riding toward a pixel-sorted rainbow pumpkin made of data, blue sky, dust, hard color split.
- Trick-or-treater silhouette reaching into a glowing portal that is an exploding crystal eye.
- Classical bust wearing a glitching skull mask, circuit projection on the marble, chrome ring halo.

## Cut and motion notes (for the edit pass)

- Hard cut on every downbeat. Hero radial on the one, grid on the three.
- Radial zoom-in on hero frames, synced to the kick. Grid frames hold still and let the cells mutate on hi-hats.
- Datamosh the transition only when the track drops. Everywhere else the glitch lives inside the object.
- Mood check for every frame: would this read as a party, not a funeral? If no, raise the neon and cut the shadows.
